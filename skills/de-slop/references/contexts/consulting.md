# Context profile: consulting

## When this fires

Consulting deliverables: client emails, executive summaries, narrative slides, slide decks, commercial proposals, project reports, steering committee documents, LinkedIn posts by consultants.

## Register

Neutral, factual, adapted to the audience and mission objective. No empty superlatives, no hollow consulting speak. Clarity and information density over style. Every statement earns its place with a fact or a concrete recommendation. The partner test: "Would this survive a senior partner or client challenge?"

## Format and channel

### Client email

Opening: go straight to the subject. No throat-clearing.

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Suite a notre echange, je reviens vers vous afin de..." | "Comme convenu, voici [X]." |
| "Je me permets de vous contacter concernant..." | [Go directly to the subject] |
| "J'espere que ce message vous trouve en bonne forme." | [Delete] |
| "Je reviens vers vous suite a notre conversation de [date]." | "Lors de notre appel de [date], nous avons conclu [X]." |
| "Please find attached..." | "[Nom du fichier] en piece jointe." |
| "I hope this email finds you well." | [Delete] |

Closing: one concrete next step, not two lines of politeness.

- "N'hesitez pas a me contacter pour tout complement d'information." -> Delete or replace with a concrete action: "Je suis disponible lundi si vous souhaitez en discuter."
- "En esperant que ce retour vous sera utile" -> Delete.
- "Restant a votre disposition pour tout echange complementaire" -> Delete.
- "Dans l'attente de votre retour" -> "Retour attendu pour [date] si possible."

Structure: subject line (what the reader must do or know), one context sentence if needed, main content (result, recommendation, question), concrete next step, signature.

### Executive summary

- No macro-context openers: "Dans un contexte de [macro-trend], [client] fait face a [generic challenge]." -> Go to the diagnosis.
- No self-promotion openers: "Forts de notre expertise en [domaine], nous proposons..." -> Go to the proposal.
- No meta-openers: "Cette etude vise a..." / "Ce document a pour objectif de..." -> State the result.
- Three identical-length bullets as summary -> Vary lengths or rewrite in prose.
- "Contexte / Enjeux / Recommandations" as sole narrative structure -> Add a central thesis, a connecting thread.
- Closing with "[Cabinet] accompagne [client] dans cette demarche." -> Delete. Close on the result, not the commercial relationship.
- Density rule: a 1-page executive summary = maximum 3 distinct ideas. More than 3 = it is a summary, not an executive summary.

### Narrative slides

Slide titles: subject + verb + concrete consequence.

| Bad (label) | Good (assertion) |
|---|---|
| "Analyse de l'existant" | "Le SI actuel bloque la montee en charge" |
| "Recommandations" | "Trois decisions a prendre avant septembre" |
| "Benchmark marche" | "Les concurrents ont deux ans d'avance sur l'IA generative" |
| "Roadmap" | "Six mois pour livrer les premiers resultats mesurables" |

Body:
- Bullet without a verb = label, not an idea. Rewrite as action sentence.
- Three same-length bullets = AI pattern. Vary or consolidate in prose.
- Body that repeats the title in longer form -> Delete redundancy. The body develops, it does not paraphrase.

Conclusion slide:
- "En conclusion, [repetition of content]..." -> Delete. The conclusion brings a decision or next step, not a summary.
- "Merci de votre confiance" as last slide -> Replace with the next step.

### LinkedIn post (consultant)

Hooks to avoid:

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Avez-vous deja remarque que [generic observation] ?" | [Direct assertion about the observation] |
| "J'ai realise quelque chose d'important recemment." | [State what was realized] |
| "Voici ce que j'ai appris en [X annees] de [domaine]." | [State the learning] |
| "Unpopular opinion:" | [State the opinion] |
| "Hot take:" | [State the point of view] |

Artificial cliffhangers:
- "La reponse m'a surpris." -> Delete. Give the answer.
- "Et ce que j'ai decouvert a change ma facon de travailler." -> Delete. State the change.
- "Suite dans les commentaires." (unless format requires it) -> Integrate in the post.

Closing: maximum one question, direct. No series of rhetorical questions. No "Likez si vous etes d'accord".

Length: hook in 1-2 short lines, body in 3-5 paragraphs, close in 1 line or 1 question. No 5+ bullet lists in a narrative post: rewrite in prose or reduce to 3.

### Commercial proposal

- No macro-context openers: "Dans un monde en constante transformation, [client] doit faire face a..." -> Start with the client's concrete situation.
- No complexity inflation: "Les enjeux de [secteur] n'ont jamais ete aussi complexes." -> Delete.
- No credential openers: "Fort de X annees d'experience, [cabinet]..." -> Delete.
- No vague value: "Nous vous accompagnons dans votre transformation." -> Say what changes concretely.
- No self-qualified approach: "Notre approche sur-mesure..." -> Describe the approach, do not qualify it.
- No abstract value claims: "[Cabinet] apporte une valeur ajoutee unique..." -> State what the client gains precisely.
- Deliverables: do not list as labels ("Rapport / Atelier / Recommandations") -> Name what they produce: "Diagnostic de maturite IA avec scoring par domaine."

## What "done" looks like

- No throat-clearing openers, no closing platitudes.
- Every slide title is an assertion, not a label.
- Every bullet has a verb.
- No self-referential meta-commentary ("ce document vise a...").
- The partner test passes: a senior partner would not need to rewrite before sending to the client.
